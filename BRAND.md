# Thirteen Month Technology — Brand Guidelines

Derived from the official logo lockup. Use this file when extending the website, presentations, or partner materials.

## Brand idea

Institutional technology. Premium, secure, international. African in origin and globally positioned. Not consumer fintech, not crypto, not playful startup.

**Message:** Building Trusted Digital Infrastructure for a Connected World.

**Proof line:** Finance · Government · Aviation · Cross-Border Verification

**Operating idea:** Verify. Connect. Manage. Audit.

## Logo

The official lockup is a three-dimensional metallic **13**, an orbital swoosh that ends in a cluster of digital squares, the words **THIRTEEN MONTH**, a ruled **TECHNOLOGY** line, and the tagline **DIGITAL TRUSTED INFRASTRUCTURE**.

### Colour roles in the mark

- **1** and **MONTH**: electric / metallic blue
- **3** and **THIRTEEN**: metallic silver
- **Swoosh and pixels**: brand blue
- **Native field**: black

### Clear space and backgrounds

- Prefer the full lockup on black or near-black.
- On light surfaces, either place the lockup on a dark plaque or use the isolated 13 mark with the wordmark set in type.
- Do not recolour the 1/3 relationship.
- Do not add drop shadows, outlines, or clip-art effects.
- Do not place the lockup on busy photography without a dark scrim.

### Animation

Motion should feel precise, not decorative:

- The swoosh may draw along its path.
- Pixels may appear in sequence at the end of the swoosh.
- A slow orbital ring and a single metallic sheen are acceptable.
- Honour `prefers-reduced-motion`.

### Light mode

Cool white / platinum ground. Ink text. Blue for actions. Silver becomes slate for contrast.

### Dark mode

Near-black / deep navy ground. Silver and white text. Blue glow on primary actions. This is the native environment of the logo.

## Typography

- **Display:** Plus Jakarta Sans — geometric, close to the lockup.
- **Body:** Source Sans 3 — readable, institutional.
- Headlines can be large and tight. Labels use wide tracking and uppercase, matching **TECHNOLOGY** in the logo.
- The thin ruled line that interrupts a tracked label is a recurring layout device.

## UI language

- Generous space, sharp geometry, hairline borders.
- Buttons: blue metallic gradient with a brief sheen; secondary is outline.
- Icons: thin linear strokes, optionally with a blue gradient.
- Photography: banking architecture, civic buildings, aviation, African and international cities, global connectivity. Always overlaid so the brand stays in control.
- Avoid neon grids, coin imagery, purple gradients, and loud glassmorphism.

## Voice

Calm, specific, and confident. Write for institutions. Do not over-claim. Do not explain confidential implementation.
